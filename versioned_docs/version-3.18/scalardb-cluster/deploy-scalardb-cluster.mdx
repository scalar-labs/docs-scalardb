# Deploy ScalarDB Cluster

This guide helps you configure and deploy ScalarDB Cluster across different platforms by providing a comprehensive overview of supported deployment options. You can choose from various combinations of platforms and pricing models for the approach that fits your environment and requirements.

## Choose a platform and pricing model

ScalarDB Cluster supports the following platform and pricing model types:

1. Platform
   - Amazon Elastic Kubernetes Service (EKS)
   - Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)
   - Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE)
   - Red Hat OpenShift (OpenShift)
   - minikube for development purposes only
   - Other platforms
1. Pricing model
   - Pay as you go (PAYG)
   - Bring your own license (BYOL)

Before proceeding, decide your preferred combination of platform and pricing model.

<details>
<summary>Matrix of supported combinations</summary>

| Platform       | Pricing model |
|----------------|---------------|
| EKS            | PAYG + BYOL   |
| AKS            | BYOL          |
| GKE            | PAYG + BYOL   |
| OpenShift      | BYOL          |
| minikube       | BYOL          |
| Other platform | [Contact us](https://www.scalar-labs.com/contact) |

</details>
